ENTRAPBALL VR: CO-OP CONTAINMENT

First-Person 3D fusion of Moraff's Entrap & JezzBall — Build walls to trap giant kinetic spheres, and delete blocks with your other trigger so you never get trapped!

Launching in Desktop Browser Mode: WASD = Move/Strafe, Mouse = Rotate, Left Click / Space = Place Block, Right Click / F = Delete Block.

Core Arena Rules
  • Build & Delete Blocks: Place blocks one at a time with Right Trigger (Click/Space). Pull the Left Trigger (Right-Click/F) to delete a single block if you ever wall yourself in.
  • Ball Safety Lock: You cannot spawn a block directly on top of a moving ball (reticle turns red when a ball is in the way).
  • JezzBall + Entrap Capture: Sealing empty sub-zones captures floor tiles. Boxing a ball into a tight compartment (≤9 cells) contains it!
  • 3D In-VR Restart Button: When you lose all lives, a clickable 3D button appears right in front of you in VR space so you can pull the trigger to restart immediately.
